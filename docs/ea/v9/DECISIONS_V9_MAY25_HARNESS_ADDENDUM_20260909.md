## D1. May exposed a harness problem, not only a market-strategy problem

May has two different replays:

- first pass: originally sequential/future-hidden in operation, but later review found material process drift away from the intended V9 decision philosophy;
- second pass: retrospective strategy-compliance audit after May outcomes were already known, therefore outcome-contaminated and **not validation evidence**.

The two passes produced radically different decisions and results under broadly the same high-level V9 language.

Accepted conclusion:

```text
strategy authority
!=
session compliance
```

A V9 session must therefore prove that it followed the authority, not merely state that it read the authority.

Do not promote the second-pass P/L as strategy performance. Preserve it only as evidence that discretionary implementation can drift materially when the decision process is not auditable enough.

---

## D2. Parent working belief must not become a directional veto

Parent is a working belief, not a default side that receives privileged treatment.

While flat, every serious candidate review must include both:

```text
CONTINUATION CASE: what supports the current Parent?
OPPOSITE / INVERSION CASE: what current evidence could support the other route?
```

Before entry, freeze:

```text
WHY THIS SIDE NOW?
WHY NOT THE OPPOSITE SIDE NOW?
```

A prior Parent may remain coherent after a Child stop or exit, but it does not persist by inertia. After every Child resolution, explicitly reassess Parent status from current evidence.

No trade quota or forced LONG/SHORT balance is introduced. Directional imbalance is a **self-audit signal**, not a market rule.

---

## D3. Child independence is necessary reasoning, not sufficient pitch quality

May first-pass drift often treated:

```text
new low/high
+ repair
+ failed repair
```

as enough to justify a new trade once the Child was causally distinct.

Accepted correction:

```text
INDEPENDENT CHILD
!=
WORTHWHILE PITCH
```

Every entry must answer two separate questions:

1. `Why is this Child genuinely distinct from the prior failed attempt or same auction?`
2. `Why is this particular Child worth risking on now inside the larger market context?`

Useful role evidence may include repeated hold, departure, return/reuse, failed repair, value relocation, or another functional behavior. These are descriptive examples, **not a mandatory checklist**. No minimum bar count, hold count, retest count, or elapsed time is authorized.

A single reaction can still be actionable if the broader context makes its role sufficiently meaningful. Conversely, a fully independent Child can still be a poor pitch.

---

## D4. `NO CHASE` is context discipline, not an undocumented minimum-R filter

The May first pass repeatedly rejected Parent opportunities because:

- price had already moved;
- the nearest known high/low was close;
- immediate known-room was smaller than the structural stop distance;
- OPEN ROUTE would need to continue beyond the nearest memory.

This sometimes acted as an unauthorized minimum-R veto.

Accepted interpretation:

```text
already moved != automatic no-trade
nearest memory != automatic TP
nearest-memory R != automatic entry gate
OPEN ROUTE != permission to ignore structure
```

`NO CHASE` means do not enter merely because price is moving and the trader fears missing it. It does **not** mean a Parent-Journey entry is forbidden after meaningful prior movement.

For a Parent-Journey trade, the trader must explain why the current Child still offers a meaningful falsifiable location inside a live larger route. A nearby destination may be a checkpoint rather than the end of the opportunity.

For a Local Bridge trade, the declared local destination remains central because the trade thesis itself is local.

No fixed minimum R, distance, ATR, or journey-age threshold is introduced.

---

## D5. Parent-Journey ambition must be explicit and real

V9's primary research objective is material participation in larger journeys, not accumulation of unrelated small bridge wins.

`LOCAL BRIDGE` remains valid, but it must describe the actual thesis:

> the trade is intended to resolve at a local route/destination.

It must not be selected merely because:

- the Parent is uncertain;
- the trader is uncomfortable holding;
- the nearest memory is visible;
- large-route continuation is hard to predict.

When a coherent Parent and a valid Child exist, the trader must explicitly consider `PARENT-JOURNEY PARTICIPATION` rather than silently defaulting to a Local Bridge.

This is an ambition/audit requirement, not a rule that forces every trade to be a Parent trade.

## D6. Mandatory symmetry and hidden-veto audit before entry

Before any new position opens, the session must run a short compliance audit:

```text
1. MIRROR CHECK
   If the chart evidence were directionally mirrored, would I evaluate it by the same standard?

2. HIDDEN-VETO CHECK
   Am I rejecting or accepting this trade because of an undocumented rule such as:
   - already moved too much
   - overbought/oversold
   - nearest memory too close
   - minimum implicit R
   - Parent says only one side is allowed

3. INDEPENDENCE VS QUALITY CHECK
   Have I explained both why the Child is new and why it is worth risking on?

4. HARD-SL CHECK
   Does the stop invalidate this Child rather than merely sit near price?

5. SCALE CHECK
   Does the intended journey match how I plan to manage the trade?

6. COUNTEREVIDENCE CHECK
   What is the strongest current evidence against this trade?
```

If the audit exposes inconsistent reasoning, the trader must correct the reasoning **before** entering. The correction may end in either `TRADE` or `NO TRADE`.

This is a process gate, not a market-prediction score.

## D7. Serious candidate rejections must be auditable

A `SERIOUS CANDIDATE` begins when the trader tightens from flat H1 context to M15 candidate review.

For each serious candidate, record either:

```text
TRADE
or
NO TRADE + explicit strategic reason
```

The reason must refer to current V9 authority, not an undocumented intuition.

Examples of acceptable reasoning include:

- Child is not yet functionally distinct;
- same-auction repair remains unresolved;
- Hard SL cannot be tied to actual Child falsification;
- intended scale is incoherent with the location;
- strongest counterevidence currently dominates the thesis;
- the candidate is only reaction/chase with no decision sufficiency.

`Already moved`, `doesn't feel good`, `room looks small`, or indicator state alone are insufficient unless translated into the actual structural role that makes the trade poor.

This requirement exists to expose hidden filters and missed Parent opportunities.

---

## D8. Exit decisions require layer-specific evidence

Before a discretionary exit, state which layer has deteriorated:

```text
EXECUTION DAMAGE
CHILD-ROUTE DAMAGE
PARENT/CAMPAIGN DAMAGE
```

For a Parent-Journey position, a full manual exit must cite current evidence that progression ability is materially deteriorating for the campaign being held. A micro reaction or indicator warning alone is insufficient.

After exit, freeze the Parent status separately. Do not convert the exit into a retrospective declaration that the final top/bottom was known.

---

## D9. Session-level self-audit is mandatory but must not force outcomes

At every pause/end of session record:

- LONG/SHORT trades and serious candidates considered;
- any strong one-sided imbalance and the structural reason for it;
- any candidate rejected using `NO CHASE`, route-room, or Parent language;
- whether any rejection relied on a rule not present in authority;
- whether any Local Bridge was chosen when a coherent Parent-Journey thesis was available;
- any causal-integrity incident.

Do **not** force balanced trade counts or manufacture opposite-side trades to satisfy this audit.

The harness is more explicit, but the market strategy remains discretionary.
