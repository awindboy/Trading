
# V9 Decisions

Date: `2026-09-07`
Status: `ACTIVE RESEARCH DECISIONS`
Production authority: `NONE`

## Decision V9-001 — Create a new generation

The shift from predicting direction to decision-making under uncertainty is large enough to justify a new generation.

V9 supersedes V8 as active strategy research.

V8 remains preserved evidence.

## Decision V9-002 — Do not rewrite previous versions as wrong

Earlier work was theoretically coherent and produced important positive and negative evidence.

V9 exists because repeated research exposed limits, not because previous work lacked logic.

## Decision V9-003 — Separate three problems permanently

```text
market understanding
!=
direction prediction
!=
trade quality
```

No future document may silently equate them.

## Decision V9-004 — Accept incomplete knowledge

Perfect explanation is not a prerequisite for a valid trade.

Stop analysis when decision sufficiency exists.

## Decision V9-005 — Manual replay precedes code

V9 market language must first be learned and falsified through chart replay.

Do not begin V9 by building a feature tournament.

## Decision V9-006 — Adaptive resolution is part of market reading

Relevant history may require M5/M15/H1/H4/D1 switching.

No fixed lookback has conceptual authority.

## Decision V9-007 — Role > Pattern

Local patterns have no standalone authority.

Parent/child route role matters.

## Decision V9-008 — Balance is not compression

A broad historical accepted region may be balance even when volatile.

## Decision V9-009 — Boundary crossing is not value translation

A breakout has stronger structural meaning only if market business relocates sufficiently.

## Decision V9-010 — Reaction is not rejection

A counter candle, wick or penetration is insufficient.

The later functional role matters.

## Decision V9-011 — Destination is not reversal permission

Destination is primarily a place to reassess.

## Decision V9-012 — Episode maturity is not a reversal signal

Age/maturity can affect hazard, not automatic direction.

## Decision V9-013 — Ultimate terminality is not required

A profitable child-to-parent journey can exist while the parent trend later resumes.

## Decision V9-014 — Use hierarchical route switching

Trade one justified bridge at a time.

A later bridge is a new decision.

## Decision V9-015 — Use sparse active memory

Do not map every historical node.

Retain only memories relevant to the current thesis.

## Decision V9-016 — Node importance is functional

Ask which area would falsify the current route if genuinely restored.

## Decision V9-017 — A pause is not a node

Candidate area authority must be earned through subsequent causal behavior.

## Decision V9-018 — Failed repair is a process hypothesis

Failed functional repair is useful working language.

No fixed formula is authorized.

## Decision V9-019 — Abrupt traversal is allowed as a distinct process

Do not require a clean retest in every route switch.

## Decision V9-020 — Direction correctness is not trade quality

A correct directional opinion can still be a bad trade if structural room is poor.

## Decision V9-021 — Decision Corridor is the active practical abstraction

Identify:

```text
falsification
current price
next active memory
structural room
```

before trading.

## Decision V9-022 — NOT YET is valid

Waiting is a positive decision.

Frequency must not be manufactured by forcing ambiguous trades.
