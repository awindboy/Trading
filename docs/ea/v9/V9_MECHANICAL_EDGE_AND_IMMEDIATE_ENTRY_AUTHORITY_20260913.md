This document fixes the next V9 research direction after the Arrival/Delivery Grammar simplification.

The project will first maximize and validate the **mechanical edge** of the Grammar before attempting any AI-based trading improvement.

The active research order is:


AI is not the current core-edge research program.

## 2. Immediate-entry convention is fixed for active research

For the current mechanical-refinement phase:

```text
CAUSAL MEANINGFUL ARRIVAL / DELIVERY EVENT
-> Grammar update using information known at that instant
-> if mechanically authorized, ENTER IMMEDIATELY
```

Do not add a separate LTF trigger requirement before entry.

The exact execution convention must be versioned, but the research intent is fixed:

- H4 liquidity first-touch / raid is the causal event;
- decision information is limited to what is known at that event;
- entry is immediate rather than waiting for a discretionary or optimized M1/M5/M15 pattern;
- no hindsight-best entry;
- no future open or future close may be used as the entry price.

The old convenience baseline:

```text
H4 liquidity arrival
-> wait for H1 close
-> next H1 open
```

remains historical comparator evidence only. It is not the intended final timing convention.

## 3. LTF role is execution clock, not edge source

For active research:

```text

= exact first-touch chronology
= execution-price / SL / TP chronology
= intraminute ambiguity evidence

M1 / M5 / M15

```

Do not restart a research program based on:

- LTF BOS / CHOCH;

These may look effective in selected periods but historically create a large optimization surface and repeatedly failed to establish stable V9 edge.

LTF entry refinement may be reconsidered only **after** the mechanical immediate-entry strategy is already frozen and profitable across consumed periods. At that later stage it must prove additive out-of-sample value over the immediate-entry baseline or be discarded.

## 8. Hard SL becomes the first major optimization axis

With entry timing fixed, the first high-value trading question is:

> At the immediate entry, which already-known structure most honestly invalidates the Child thesis?

Candidate factual references may include:

- causal H1 structural swing;
- response origin;
- relevant OB distal;
- delivery-leg structural invalidation;
- other objectively versioned structural references.

Rules:

- Hard SL is fixed before the Child is opened;
- never widen Hard SL;
- never restore a stopped Child;
- candidate selection must use only information known at entry;
- compare broad distributions, not one or two attractive examples.

## 9. TP and journey are the second major optimization axis

## 10. Multi-Child and exposure are third, not entry filters

Earlier consumed comparisons showed that:

- blindly opening a full-risk Child on every delivery can be poor in some periods;

Research must determine whether repeated opportunities are:

```text

or

```

without inventing retry limits or route trade quotas.

Portfolio / exposure control is a separate risk layer from market-edge discovery.
