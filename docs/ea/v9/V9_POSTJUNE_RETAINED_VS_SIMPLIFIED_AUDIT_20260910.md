## Explicitly not removed

Simplification does **not** mean:

- ignore Parent context;
- allow random re-entry after a stop;
- remove opposite-side consideration;
- remove Hard SL;
- scalp every trade;
- hold forever;
- automate discretionary good-pitch selection;
- turn V9 into an EA-only system;
- allow the AI to invent official support/resistance prices.

---

## Explicitly not added

The post-June correction does **not** add:

- `maximum 3 losses`;
- cooldown after losses;
- minimum R;
- minimum stop size;
- ATR stop;
- fixed TP;
- fixed trailing/BE/partial;
- sideways-market ban;
- fixed retry limit;
- numeric pitch score.

The purpose is to make V9 simpler to execute faithfully, not more mechanical.
