## Decision V9-053 — Quality of attempt is permanently separate from market outcome

Judge trade process and realized outcome separately.
A loss can be a good attempt; a win can be a poor attempt.
Do not optimize research toward making every individual outcome look correct.

## Decision V9-054 — Do not regress into direction-accuracy research by default

The project has repeatedly observed unstable / near-chance broad mandatory direction.
Future V9 work must not silently redefine success as finding the final direction oracle.
New direction research requires genuinely new causal information and an explicit reason it improves a trader decision.

## Decision V9-055 — Adopt the baseball-hitter mindset as active research philosophy

The trader cannot know whether a good swing becomes a home run, single, or out.
The controllable object is opportunity selection, execution quality, falsification, discipline, and continued participation when the market offers more.

## Decision V9-056 — Market movement and trade opportunity are separate

The market is always moving, but every movement is not our pitch.
Do not trade merely because direction appears obvious.
Do not require certainty or a known final destination before every trade.

## Decision V9-057 — Parent Journey is a working belief, not a trend classifier

Do not convert Parent Journey into a fixed UPTREND/DOWNTREND/RANGE oracle.
It exists to preserve continuity of market understanding across local trade resolution.
Update it when newly revealed behavior materially changes the larger story.

## Decision V9-058 — Child invalidation does not automatically invalidate Parent Journey

Permanent distinction:

```text
invalidated child thesis
!=
invalidated parent journey
```

A local trade can fail while the larger route remains coherent.

## Decision V9-059 — Child success does not prove Parent Journey direction

Permanent distinction:

```text
winning child trade
!=
proof of parent continuation/reversal
```

Counterflow child trades can succeed inside a larger opposing journey.

## Decision V9-060 — Add Parent Ledger and Child Trade Ledger as separate discretionary records

Parent Ledger changes only when larger understanding changes materially.
Child Trade Ledger records local entry, falsification, route, and resolution.
Every child resolution must state whether it materially changed the parent story.

## Decision V9-061 — Large trends may be traded as campaigns rather than single predictions

A +100 or larger GOLD journey does not need to be predicted or held from exact origin to exact termination.
V9 may participate through multiple independently justified child opportunities under one continuing parent campaign.

## Decision V9-062 — Do not let local checkpoint logic terminate parent understanding

A child memory may resolve a child trade while remaining only a transit structure inside the parent journey.
Future research must distinguish parent memory, child/transit memory, and execution memory by role rather than timeframe label alone.

## Decision V9-063 — Continuity architecture is now a primary V9 research problem

The deeper limitation exposed by January replay is not only exit distance.
It is the loss of market continuity after each local trade resolves.
Study how parent belief survives, is damaged, repairs, or changes while local opportunities come and go.

## Decision V9-064 — Do not use Parent Journey to widen local risk mechanically

A large desired movement does not authorize an H1/H4-sized stop.
Preserve near meaningful falsification where it is genuinely thesis-dependent.
Local entry precision and larger parent opportunity are separate scales that must be connected causally, not by wider stops.

## Decision V9-065 — Anti-drift review is mandatory before formalizing a new V9 rule

Before adding a rule/model/threshold, explicitly check whether the project is:

- searching for certainty rather than decision quality;
- fitting one example;
- converting description into objective truth;
- confusing child outcome with parent state;
- ignoring open opportunity because final destination is unknown;
- forcing a trade because a large move is visible.

If so, return to replay instead of formalizing.

## Decision V9-066 — Parent Journey Anatomy is outcome-informed archaeology only

The January 2025/2026 Parent Journey anatomy documents demonstrate that large H1/H4-scale journeys contained many local V9 memories and missed continuation opportunities.
They do not define a trend algorithm and have no validation authority.

## Decision V9-067 — GOLD# 2021 remains locked

The parent-journey / continuity language is new and changing.
Do not contaminate the final reserve while this contract is immature.
