## 4. Primary prospective hypotheses

### H1 — Auditable symmetry reduces directional process drift

Question:

> Does forcing the trader to state both the Parent continuation case and the opposite/inversion case reduce unexplained one-sided behavior without forcing artificial LONG/SHORT balance?

Evidence to collect:

- side distribution of serious candidates;
- side distribution of actual trades;
- `WHY THIS SIDE / WHY NOT OPPOSITE` rationale;
- mirror-check failures or revisions.

No target LONG/SHORT ratio is authorized.

### H2 — Separating Child independence from pitch quality reduces low-quality attempts

Question:

> Does explicitly requiring `why is this worth risking on?` improve decision quality beyond merely proving that the Child is new?

Seek both:

- independent Child that is correctly rejected as poor pitch;
- simple-looking Child that is accepted because its role/context is genuinely strong.

Do not convert role examples into a checklist.

### H3 — Removing hidden `NO CHASE`/minimum-R vetoes restores large-journey availability without creating impulsive chase

Question:

> Can V9 remain available for a developed Parent opportunity after prior movement, while still rejecting entries driven only by fear of missing out?

Record every serious candidate where `already moved`, `nearest memory`, `journey maturity`, or route room materially influences the decision.

The rationale must state structural meaning, not an implicit threshold.

### H4 — Intended journey scale becomes behaviorally real

Question:

> When a coherent Parent and meaningful Child exist, does the trader actually participate as a Parent-Journey trade rather than silently collapsing to local bridge management?

Record:

- declared scale;
- why that scale fits the thesis;
- whether management later drifted to another scale;
- large-journey participation/capture after retrospective Parent resolution.

### H5 — Campaign-health exits remain useful under the stricter audit

Question:

> Can the trader protect meaningful progress without overreacting to ordinary counterflow?

Every full Parent-Journey manual exit must identify:

- failing layer;
- favorable-extreme behavior;
- settlement migration;
- counterflow business;
- route repair outcome.

No fixed profit-lock rule is authorized.
