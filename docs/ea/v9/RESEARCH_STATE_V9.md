## 3. Current strongest qualitative findings

### Adaptive resolution

Market history should be read across changing timeframes instead of fixed bar counts.

### Role > Pattern

The parent role of a local event often matters more than its visible label.

### Market value migration

Boundary crossing alone does not establish a new route.

Where business/accepted centers rebuild matters.

### Reaction versus rejection

Opposite movement is common.

Functional inability to restore/rebuild a prior structure matters more.

### Hierarchical route switching

A child route can fail and open a journey toward a parent node while the larger parent trend later survives.

Ultimate top/bottom prediction is unnecessary.

### Sparse active memory

Only a few historical objects normally matter to the current decision.

### Counterfactual thesis dependence

The relevant memory is often the one whose restoration would negate the active route interpretation.

### Structural asymmetry

Good trade quality may come from:

```text
near meaningful falsification
+
farther natural destination
```

rather than exceptional direction confidence.
