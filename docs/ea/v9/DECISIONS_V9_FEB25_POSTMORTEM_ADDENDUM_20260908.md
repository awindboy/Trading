## V9-079 — Close stop alone does not define a good location

A journey entry is high quality only when the close local falsification is also meaningful to the larger opportunity and meaningful larger room exists if correct.

## V9-080 — Scale Alignment becomes an active discretionary research concept

`SCALE ALIGNMENT` describes whether local execution invalidation meaningfully overlaps with child/parent thesis invalidation.
It is not a score, threshold, or mechanical gate.

## V9-081 — Intended Journey Scale must be frozen before entry

Every future V9 entry must state whether it is primarily `LOCAL BRIDGE` or `PARENT-JOURNEY PARTICIPATION`.
This prevents a parent-journey thesis from silently becoming a nearest-memory scalp after entry.

## V9-082 — Initial risk may not be widened to survive ordinary noise

If the intended larger thesis can survive ordinary movement through the proposed local stop, do not widen the stop after entry. The correct response is to seek a better scale-aligned entry location.

## V9-083 — Structure authority is hierarchical

Distinguish `EXECUTION ANCHOR`, `CHILD ORIGIN`, `CHILD TRANSIT MEMORY`, `PARENT FAILURE AREA`, and `PARENT MAJOR ARRIVAL`.
A child transit memory is not automatically a full-position TP or campaign invalidation.

## V9-084 — New child anchor does not automatically become full-position stop authority

The loss of a new micro/child anchor must be interpreted according to the scale it actually damages.
Do not mechanically trail the entire position behind every earned child anchor.

## V9-085 — Broad-auction interior failed repair has reduced strategic authority

P017-P020 show that repeated local failures inside a two-sided broad auction can create churn.
Before a new child is treated as independent, ask whether genuinely new market information/value migration exists.
No numeric auction classifier is authorized.

## V9-086 — OPEN ROUTE analysis must include journey maturity

`Known memory consumed + no destination + close anchor` is insufficient.
Record whether the context is fresh launch, established journey, late extension, or damaged/repair as shadow language only.

## V9-087 — Nearest memory is not automatically terminal

For a parent-journey trade, the first child transit memory can cause reaction without resolving the entire opportunity.
CP1 full exit remains historical comparison control, not universal lifecycle authority.

## V9-088 — Passive holding is not the correction

The prior neutral passive-runner audit remains negative and P011 remains a shock/capture-latency counterexample.
The correction is structure-scale authority, not `hold longer no matter what`.

## V9-089 — Parent Journey must change trading ambition

If the parent remains coherent after a child resolution or a missed leg, V9 must remain available for the next meaningful scale-aligned opportunity.
Do not chase, but do not silently reset the campaign to zero.
