# V9 January 2025 vs January 2026 Deep Review

Status: DEVELOPMENT REVIEW / NOT VALIDATION
Market: GOLD#
Method: discretionary causal replay / Decision Corridor

## 1. Executive conclusion

The January transfer test does **not** establish strategy robustness.

What transfers reasonably well is the *market-reading discipline*:

- direction correctness != trade quality;
- reaction != rejection;
- boundary crossing != value translation;
- destination != automatic reversal;
- later same-direction movement cannot rescue an invalidated thesis;
- abrupt traversal alone has no continuation authority.

What does **not** yet transfer is trade production and performance.

2025 January produced one actual trade and one clean structural loss (~-1.04R) despite many large directional moves.
2026 January development replay produced seven actual trades: six destination-resolved winners and one weekend-gap-censored failure. Winner R values were ~+1.36, +2.29, +1.95, +1.84, +1.42 plus the pre-existing +1.52 process rehearsal; the gap failure has only a non-executable first-print reference of ~-3.36R.

Therefore current status should be stated as:

> V9 shows promising *conditional trade geometry* in some 2026 episodes, but no evidence yet that the strategy has period-robust positive expectancy.

## 3. What appears genuinely strong in the method

### A. Direction and trade quality are successfully separated

Both years contain many cases where the eventual direction was readable but the entry was rejected because the first memory was too close.

This is a real improvement over pattern/direction chasing.

The method repeatedly avoided rewriting a NO TRADE as a missed winner merely because price later continued.

### B. Waiting for repair can improve geometry

Best example: 2026 PAPER-003.

The initial breakdown was not chased. A repair toward the broken ~4600 memory created a much closer counterfactual falsification and ~2.29R room to the first lower destination.

2026 PAPER-007 has the same hierarchical logic: the large ~5595 scar alone was not the entry; a later smaller child repair around ~5529 created the closer anchor.

This suggests a potentially important principle:

> movement creates information; repair can convert that information into asymmetric trade location.

This is more meaningful than a generic 'break and retest' pattern because the role of the repaired area depends on parent/child context and actual value restoration.

### C. Memory role inversion is useful

2026 PAPER-003 treated ~4600 as bearish restoration/falsification.
PAPER-004 later treated the same area as a long anchor only after genuine restoration and acceptance.

This supports Role > Pattern and argues against permanent support/resistance labels.

### D. Thesis-based management prevented arbitrary P/L decisions

PAPER-003 survived a near-full repair to ~4601.91 and later reached destination.
PAPER-005 briefly penetrated its anchor to ~4597.8 but recovered without rebuilding lower value and later reached destination.

The process did not use automatic BE, arbitrary partials, or target extension.

### E. The 2025 loss was not rescued by hindsight

2025 PAPER-001 genuinely rebuilt below the frozen ~2649-2650 base and was exited at ~2649.32 for ~-1.04R.
Price later rallied beyond the original target.

The journal correctly treated this as a new episode rather than retroactively declaring the stop wrong.

This is important causal hygiene.

## 4. The most serious weaknesses / falsification risks

### A. Strategy robustness is currently weak

One month produced 7 trades with apparently strong results; another full month produced 1 losing trade.

This is not robustness. At best it suggests strong regime selectivity.

A strategy can legitimately abstain in unsuitable regimes, but then the research question changes from:

> Does V9 work across periods?

to:

> Can V9 causally recognize, before outcomes, when its trade geometry exists and when it does not?

That second question is not yet answered independently.

### B. The method may be 'known-memory bridge trading', not a complete market strategy

2025 January rose ~6.27%, and 2026 also had huge price-discovery runs that V9 intentionally did not trade because no causal destination existed above.

Thus the present method is structurally weak at monetizing price discovery.

This is not a reason to invent targets above unseen structure. It means price discovery should be recognized as a separate unresolved trade family rather than silently treated as a weakness of the existing corridor.

### C. 'Genuine restoration' remains the largest subjectivity risk

The difference between a touch, penetration, settlement and genuine restoration materially changes outcomes.

Examples:

- 2026 PAPER-003: highs reached ~4601.91 inside the 4600-4602 falsification area, but value did not establish above it; trade survived and won.
- 2026 PAPER-005: low reached ~4597.8 below a 4598.5-4599 anchor, but value did not rebuild below; trade survived and won.
- 2025 PAPER-001: consecutive M1 closes moved below the 2649-2650 base, so the thesis was invalidated and lost.

These examples can be internally coherent, but the current language leaves enough freedom that another session could classify them differently.

Therefore the next maturity step is **not** a numeric threshold. It is to make every pre-entry journal state what observable behavior would constitute genuine restoration on the relevant resolution, before future is revealed.

### D. 'First active memory' can become an overly conservative escape hatch

2025 contained many large moves that were rejected because an intervening memory was close.
Sometimes that was correct; e.g. Jan-27/28 armed short was eventually restored above its expected cap without becoming a trade.
But other NO TRADE episodes later traversed the nearby memory and continued far.

The unresolved question is:

> When is an intervening memory still destination authority, and when has it been sufficiently consumed that the next memory is the true destination?

This matters directly to R and trade frequency.

2026 PAPER-004 itself acknowledges this: 4608-4612 was an intervening memory, yet the route to 4618 was allowed.
2025 C026 rejected a long because 2679-2692 was treated as dense node-internal travel.

If this distinction is not reproducible, destination selection can unintentionally become outcome-favorable discretion.

### E. Parent context may currently be partly narrative rather than decision authority

PAPER-003 and PAPER-006 were shorts inside a bullish Daily/H4 parent.
PAPER-004 and PAPER-005 were longs with the bullish parent.

This is acceptable because parent context was never intended as a gate. But it raises a research question: does parent context actually alter the chosen child, falsification, destination, or uncertainty? If not, it risks becoming decorative narrative.

Future reviews should explicitly record **what parent context changed in the decision**, if anything.

### F. Structural falsification is not executable risk

PAPER-006 is the strongest negative result.

The structural anchor was nearby, but a weekend gap reopened far beyond it. Therefore:

> nearby structural falsification != nearby realized loss

The Decision Corridor is incomplete unless the uncertainty assessment includes whether the falsification can actually be observed and executed continuously before a known market discontinuity.

This should not become a hindsight Friday veto. It is a general execution-continuity dimension.

### G. Current 2026 result is highly vulnerable to contamination

2026 was already qualitatively exposed before this replay. The six winners cannot be treated as independent evidence of a 6/7 winning strategy.

2025 also has project-level contamination risk from earlier generations.

Therefore the 2025-vs-2026 comparison is development transfer, not validation.

## 5. What the two months suggest about the actual edge

The best current hypothesis is narrower than 'market memory predicts price'.

A more defensible working hypothesis is:

> A known structural role change becomes tradeable when recent translation creates a nearby counterfactual falsification and the next genuinely active memory remains materially farther away. Repair behavior often creates the usable entry location.

The possible edge is therefore not the memory itself.
It may be the combination of:

1. role change / translation;
2. failed or successful repair;
3. local counterfactual anchor;
4. remaining structural room;
5. execution continuity.

This hypothesis survived several 2026 examples but failed at least once structurally in 2025 and once through execution discontinuity in 2026.

That is exactly the kind of mixed evidence needed before formalization.

## 6. Recommended research changes — without inventing a new strategy

### Keep unchanged

- Decision Corridor core
- parent/child hierarchy
- first natural destination discipline
- no automatic BE/partial/fixed runner
- destination -> resolve -> reassess
- no later-move rescue after invalidation
- no look-ahead

### Tighten the research journal

Before each future trade, explicitly freeze:

1. `Anchor zone`
2. `What observable behavior counts as genuine restoration/invalidation`
3. `First intervening memory`
4. `Why that memory is active or already consumed`
5. `First natural destination`
6. `Execution-continuity uncertainty`
7. `What parent context actually changes in this decision`

This remains qualitative and causal; it is not a threshold/score/EA.

### Retrospective trade-family tags only

Use descriptive tags to keep unlike mechanisms separate:

- RESTORATION / ROLE-INVERSION BRIDGE
- FAILED-REPAIR CONTINUATION BRIDGE
- PRICE-DISCOVERY / NO KNOWN DESTINATION
- NODE-INTERNAL / BALANCE
- EXECUTION-DISCONTINUITY CASE

Do not use these tags as entry gates yet.

### Highest-priority matched-pair research

1. PAPER-003 / PAPER-007 versus PAPER-006
   - all are failed-repair shorts;
   - separate structural quality from execution risk.

2. PAPER-004 / PAPER-005 versus 2025 PAPER-001
   - all involve restoration / repaired base behavior;
   - compare what made restoration durable versus false.

3. 2026 PAPER-004 versus 2025 C026
   - study when an intervening memory blocks the route versus is already consumed.

4. 2025/2026 price-discovery NO TRADE runs
   - study separately; do not retrofit the known-memory corridor with invented destinations.

## 7. Research decision after this review

Do **not** call V9 robust yet.
Do **not** retune thresholds to improve 2025.
Do **not** discard V9 because 2025 produced one loss.
Do **not** use GOLD# 2021 yet.

The rational next stage is more period-transfer replay under the same qualitative contract, while tightening pre-entry descriptions enough that another session can independently reproduce the falsification and destination choices.

Only after multiple additional wins, losses and no-trade months should we ask whether the observed trade families have stable positive expectancy and whether price-discovery requires a separate architecture.
