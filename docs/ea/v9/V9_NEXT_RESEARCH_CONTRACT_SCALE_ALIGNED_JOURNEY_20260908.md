## 1. Purpose

Test the post-February strategy revision without turning the February review into an overfit rule set.

Primary goal:

> Enter with local precision where local failure is meaningful to a larger opportunity, then manage the position at the scale of the intended journey rather than automatically at the scale of the newest micro structure.

Do not seek perfect direction prediction.

---

## 2. Pre-entry market read

Before every possible trade state:

1. Parent Journey working belief.
2. Strongest evidence supporting it.
3. Strongest damage/counterflow.
4. Whether value is genuinely migrating or both directions keep repairing inside one broad auction.
5. Current child route.
6. Active memories by role, not merely by distance.
7. Intended Journey Scale: `LOCAL BRIDGE` or `PARENT-JOURNEY PARTICIPATION`.
8. Local execution anchor.
9. Why losing that anchor would or would not materially damage the intended larger thesis.
10. First known memory / OPEN ROUTE context.
11. Uncertainty.

---

## 3. Scale Alignment working test

Do not use a numeric score.

Ask:

```text
If this local anchor fails as ordinary noise,
would I still believe essentially the same H1/H4 opportunity exists?
```

If clearly yes, the entry is weakly aligned for a parent-journey trade.
Wait for a better location rather than planning to widen risk later.

A close anchor is valuable only when it carries real thesis information.

## 8. Position management

Never widen the frozen initial accounting risk to rescue a trade.

During an open parent-journey trade separate:

```text
execution damage
child-route damage
parent/campaign damage
```

When a new micro anchor fails, do NOT automatically exit the entire position.
Ask what scale of thesis was invalidated.

But do not use this principle to ignore genuine rapid deterioration. P011 remains the mandatory shock counterexample.

No fixed partial percentages, break-even rules, or ATR trails are authorized.

---

## 9. Memory roles

Use:

```text
EXECUTION ANCHOR
CHILD ORIGIN
CHILD TRANSIT MEMORY
PARENT FAILURE AREA
PARENT MAJOR ARRIVAL
```

The nearest child transit memory can be a reaction point without being a mandatory full exit.

If a trade is explicitly `LOCAL BRIDGE`, a child destination may legitimately resolve it. Do not misreport that as parent-journey capture.

---

## 10. After child resolution

If the parent journey survives:

```text
child resolved
!=
campaign over
```

Remain available.

Do not chase a move already underway.
Wait for a new meaningful repair / scale-aligned location.

A new entry must contain genuinely new market information; parent survival by itself is never entry permission.

## 12. Review cadence optimization

Preserve causal integrity while reducing non-market overhead.

When flat and no candidate is near:
- use coarser context reveals appropriate to market activity.

When a candidate is forming:
- tighten to M15 or the minimum cadence needed to decide causally.

When in position:
- review often enough to evaluate the declared lifecycle scale;
- use finer M1/M5 only around actual falsification/ambiguity.

Never backfill a trade because a coarse reveal exposed a move.

Do NOT add:

- retry limits;
- minimum 3R/5R rule;
- fixed point or ATR journey maturity threshold;
- fixed auction-width threshold;
- numeric scale-alignment score;

- passive hold-until-parent-death rule;
- fixed partial ratio;
- fixed trailing-stop multiple.

---

## 14. Research populations to seek deliberately

Seek both positive and negative examples of:

- strong scale alignment that still loses;
- weak scale alignment that wins;
- broad auction with no good pitch;
- broad auction edge trade that works;
- same-auction repeated thesis failure;
- genuine new business after failure;
- fresh OPEN ROUTE winner;
- late OPEN ROUTE winner and loser;
- child anchor failure with parent continuation;
- child anchor failure that genuinely precedes parent failure;
- large winner where larger-scale management helps;
- shock case where holding larger gives back too much;
- missed leg followed by good campaign re-entry;
- parent journey with zero good local entry.
