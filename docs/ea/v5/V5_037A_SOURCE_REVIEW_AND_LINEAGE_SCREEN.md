## Gold external-state review

A materially different branch exists outside chart-only state.

Evidence:
- World Gold Council research documents that US real yields and the US dollar can explain an important part of gold-price variation, while explicitly warning that the relationships change across regimes and can flip;
- Smales & Yang (2015) find gold futures returns/volatility/activity respond quickly to US macroeconomic announcements, with response depending on surprise/belief dispersion;
- Sobti, Sehgal & Ilango (2021) find US macro news materially affects international gold price discovery and that the effect is asymmetric and state-dependent;
- the Federal Reserve H.15 release provides a daily 10-year TIPS real-yield series (`DFII10`), but the observation is published with a lag, so point-in-time availability must be respected.

This does NOT establish a trade rule.

It supports a new falsifiable question:

```text
Does a causally known change in US real-yield state
condition the next GOLD day's directional delivery distribution?
```

## Why the first V5-037A test uses real yield only

The Federal Reserve H.10 broad dollar index is released weekly on Monday at 4:15pm ET for the previous business week.
Using its daily observation labels as though they were available each day would create look-ahead.

Therefore V5-037A does not combine the daily H.10 dollar index into the first mechanism test.

A dollar variable may be added only in a later preregistered experiment using genuinely causal availability or a separately justified live-price proxy.

## Important non-equivalence

H.15 `DFII10` is a market-yield summary, not a surprise announcement that creates new information at 4:15pm.

V5-037A therefore treats it as a **slow external state measurement**. It does not claim the H.15 release itself causes a gold move.

The initial test is mechanism discovery only. Entry/SL/TP are prohibited until the external-state relation survives its frozen falsifier.
