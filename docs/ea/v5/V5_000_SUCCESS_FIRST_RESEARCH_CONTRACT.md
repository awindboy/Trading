# V5-000 — Success-First Research Contract

Status: `PRE-REGISTERED ACTIVE DISCOVERY CONTRACT`
Date: `2026-08-27`

## 1. Research premise

The fact that many naive implementations of technical analysis fail does not imply that every successful
technical trader is exploiting a magic visual shape.

V5 tests a different premise:

> Long-lived successful technical traders may be using chart patterns as compressed observations of deeper
> state, participant, volatility, liquidity, and payoff mechanisms.

The project will reverse-engineer those mechanisms before building the next strategy.

## 2. Inclusion rule for trader corpus

A trader/program enters the primary corpus only if at least one is present:

- multi-decade institutional or managed-account history;
- third-party performance/ranking evidence;
- documented live-capital experiment;
- long public proprietary history with sufficiently specific process/performance disclosure.

Sources must distinguish:
- primary statement;
- third-party evidence;
- secondary retelling.

Fame is not evidence.

## 3. Extraction template

For every trader:

```text
success evidence
market / horizon
what they actually observe
what pattern names they use
what they say the pattern represents
state/context requirements
trigger / confirmation
invalidation
exit / winner treatment
risk / sizing
adaptation through time
mechanism hypothesis
falsifier
what NOT to import
```

## 4. No strategy promotion from narrative

A compelling explanation is not evidence of expectancy.

A mechanism must progress:

```text
practitioner observation
-> falsifiable causal observable
-> broad discovery population
-> stability analysis
-> pre-registered candidate
-> independent validation
```

## 5. Research target vs final project target

A successful-trader corpus may include methods with <50% win rate.

That does not relax the eventual project target of >=50% realized WR.

The corpus is used to discover mechanisms, not to copy another trader's payoff distribution exactly.

## 6. First corpus conclusion

The initial cross-trader convergence is:

```text
pattern identity is secondary;
state-conditioned resolution of market boundaries is primary.
```

This conclusion authorizes V5-001 event-ledger research only.

It does not authorize an Entry rule.
