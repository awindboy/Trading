## Academic / microstructure sources

### A-001 Carol L. Osler — Currency Orders and Exchange Rate Dynamics
- Journal of Finance 58(5), 2003.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/1540-6261.00588
- FRBNY: https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr125.html
- Evidence:
  - take-profit and stop-loss order clustering;
  - support/resistance reversal mechanism;
  - acceleration after crossing via stop-loss clustering.
- V5 use: real microstructure basis for boundary interaction.

### A-002 Cont, Kukanov, Stoikov — Price Impact of Order Book Events
- Journal of Financial Econometrics / working-paper lineage.
- Summary: https://www.sashastoikov.com/finance/2015/3/12/the-price-impact-of-order-book-events
- Evidence:
  - short-horizon price changes strongly related to order-flow imbalance;
  - impact slope inversely related to market depth.
- V5 use: formal bridge from `effort/result` intuition to measurable price impact.

### A-003 Moskowitz, Ooi, Pedersen — Time Series Momentum
- Journal of Financial Economics 104(2), 2012.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2011.11.003
- Evidence:
  - time-series momentum across 58 liquid futures/forwards;
  - 1-12 month return persistence.
- V5 use: trend persistence is a real empirical object, not just practitioner folklore.

### A-004 Hurst, Ooi, Pedersen — A Century of Evidence on Trend-Following Investing
- URL: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2993026
- Evidence: trend-following evidence back to 1880 across regimes.
- V5 use: longevity of expansion/persistence harvesting.

### A-005 Hong & Stein — Underreaction, Momentum Trading, Overreaction
- Journal of Finance.
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00184
- V5 use: one behavioral mechanism for gradual information diffusion / momentum.
- Note: theory does not prove any particular chart pattern.

### A-006 Volatility clustering literature
- Example review/stylized fact summary:
  https://ojs.victoria.ac.nz/nzref/article/view/1735
- V5 use: low/high volatility states persist.
- Critical caveat: volatility clustering alone does NOT prove `contraction -> imminent expansion`.
## V5-001A adversarial/source addendum — 2026-08-27

### A-007 Lo, Mamaysky, Wang — Foundations of Technical Analysis
- Journal of Finance 2000 / NBER WP 7613.
- https://www.nber.org/papers/w7613
- Several chart-pattern classes carried incremental distributional information in historical U.S. equities.
- V5 lesson: pattern information can exist, but shape recognition alone does not identify economic mechanism or
  guarantee tradable expectancy.

### A-008 Kavajecz & Odders-White — Technical Analysis and Liquidity Provision
- Review of Financial Studies 17(4), 2004.
- DOI: 10.1093/rfs/hhg057
- Reported support/resistance levels coinciding with limit-order-book depth peaks.
- V5 lesson: some technical levels can correspond to liquidity structure, but this does not make every convenient
  prior high/low such a level.

### A-009 Marshall, Cahan & Cahan — Does intraday technical analysis in the U.S. equity market have value?
- Journal of Empirical Finance 15(2), 2008.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jempfin.2006.05.003
- 7,846 popular intraday technical rules showed no profitability after data-snooping correction.
- V5 lesson: success-first mechanism work still requires harsh negative controls.

### A-010 Bajgrowicz & Scaillet — Technical trading revisited
- Journal of Financial Economics 106(3), 2012.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.jfineco.2012.06.001
- Historical technical-rule discoveries did not persist ex ante and low transaction costs erased performance.
- V5 lesson: apparent rule discovery and exploitable persistent edge are different questions.
