## Why phases 7 and 8 of the backlog stop here

The entry-method selector was explicitly conditional on a credible state that
could choose among scalp/swing/trend management. Proof speed, H1 acceptance,
H1 path, morphology, HASTOC and proof history did not produce such a stable
state. Defining pullback/breakout execution now would introduce a new strategy
and new degrees of freedom rather than test the stated mechanism.

Sizing was explicitly last. No state improved loss count, win rate, block
quality and post-cutoff direction strongly enough to authorize larger size.
