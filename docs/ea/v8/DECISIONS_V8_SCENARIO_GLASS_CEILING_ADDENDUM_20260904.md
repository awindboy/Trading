## Decision 1 — Active research moves from grid rescue to scenario portfolio

The completed static sizing/deep-action grid work remains preserved history/control.

The active research question is now whether P15 can supply a broad movement-event universe that is routed into multiple semantically distinct scenario modules.

Do not continue HOLD/EXIT/hedge threshold tournaments without a genuinely new mechanism.

## Decision 2 — Preserve V3's architecture, not V3's strict serial Entry chain

V3's valuable concept is:

    auction/delivery state x local event x acceptance/failure

Copying the complete V3 reaction/retest/acceptance chain into V8 recreated scarcity.

Use V3 as a scenario-routing framework, not as a mandatory Entry template.

## Decision 3 — Frequency must come from orthogonal modules

Do not broaden one profitable scenario by weakening its conditions until quality disappears.

Build frequency by adding independent causal scenario families with low overlap.

Parameter variants of the same mechanism do not count as separate modules.

## Decision 4 — Representation is not new information

Price-derived theories such as Directional Change, auction/value labels, swings, indicators and price-only clusters are information-preserving representations unless they add a genuinely new causal data source.

Do not grant them direction authority merely because the representation is intuitive.

## Decision 5 — Track information-expanding sources separately

Scheduled macro calendar, legally known macro surprise, COMEX order flow/depth and other causally available external sources belong to a distinct information-expanding lane.

Any use requires explicit known-at and latency rules.

## Decision 6 — Movement and direction remain separate targets

P15 remains movement opportunity.

Scenario direction must be defined with explicit distance and horizon.

Do not extrapolate a short-distance first-touch edge into a long runner without separate continuation evidence.

## Decision 7 — `Invariant before profitable`

Before promoting P/L, require evidence of:

- chronological stability;
- natural scale stability;
- parameter perturbation stability;
- mirror/control asymmetry;
- sufficient execution-cost margin.

Do not optimize a single-point backtest peak.

## Decision 8 — Edge margin is first-class

When cost matters, report the gross structural edge and the actual Bid/Ask edge.

A tiny positive zero-cost result that turns negative after spread is a thin edge, not a strong edge destroyed by the broker.

## Decision 9 — The current three-module portfolio is development evidence only

Current 2024 exact-tick scenario union:

- candidates 96;
- completed 89;
- positive WR ~55.1%;
- mean ~+0.105R;
- PF ~1.26;
- average positive ~+0.917R.

It improves frequency and is promising, but average winner remains below the final project goal and no independent exact-tick validation exists.

Do not retune 2024 to force the missing target.

## Decision 10 — Preserve survival / continuation separation

The V3-L-style checkpoint architecture is a useful control:

    +1R survival checkpoint
    -> partial realization
    -> residual BE
    -> separate continuation target

Do not require the initial scenario to predict a move beyond its measured lifetime.

## Decision 11 — Longer TP is not a solution

Checkpoint continuation decays materially beyond +2R in the current exact-tick union.

Do not increase runner TP simply to make average winner exceed 1R.

## Decision 12 — Directional Change is audit-first

Directional Change / intrinsic-time research may proceed only first as shadow labeling / glass-ceiling audit.

Do not optimize DC thresholds for P/L.

First test threshold sensitivity, frequency collapse, overshoot decay, chronological stability, cost sensitivity and incremental information versus current scenario states.

## Decision 13 — Auction theory is semantic language, not authority

Acceptance/rejection and failed-auction concepts may define scenario hypotheses, but each must beat naive breakout/reversal controls and survive chronology/execution.

Do not call a pattern an auction edge because the narrative is plausible.

## Decision 14 — V3 validation failure remains the mandatory warning

Future scenario candidates must be designed to be falsified, not rescued.

## Decision 17 — Broad Directional Change direction is rejected

The first shadow DC glass-ceiling audit is complete at natural `0.125S / 0.25S / 0.50S` scales.

Single-scale current direction and 3-scale alignment are near chance / chronologically unstable for the primary `+/-0.25S within 15m` target.

Outside the current scenario modules, 3-scale aligned direction is approximately `49.8% / 54.2% / 48.2%` in 2024/2025/2026.

Natural 240/480/960-minute initialization windows produce almost identical DC states, so the failure is not primarily an initialization artifact.

Do not optimize DC thresholds or add DC alignment as a generic scenario gate.

DC remains allowed only as shadow intrinsic-time representation or inside a newly preregistered causal interaction.


## Decision 18 — Do not declare price-only information exhausted

Repeated failure of generic next-bar/short-distance direction does not prove that all OHLC/price-derived information is useless.

A separate allowed target is a causal transition into a persistent state.

The project therefore keeps two live lanes:

    price-only transition/persistence
    +
    information-expanding external inputs

Neither lane has authority over the other.

## Decision 19 — External literature supports questions, not rules

Moskowitz-Ooi-Pedersen, Mesfin, Cont-Kukanov-Stoikov, Park-Irwin and White are recorded as supporting/falsification references.

## Decision 20 — Mesfin 2026 changes the next question, not the strategy

The verified MNQ preprint shows fourteen common OHLCV signal families failing a strict friction/OOS framework while two separate positive controls are regime/state-transition oriented and use longer horizons.

This is not proof that GOLD should use GMM/Markov states.

It is sufficient reason to preregister the following question:

> After P15 movement ignition, does a clean state transition form that persists long enough to create economically meaningful directional distance?

## Decision 22 — Transition quality must be incremental

Any discovered transition must be tested against:

- simple momentum/breakout controls;
- static-state controls;
- current Late Ignition / BB Persistence / Macro labels;
- chronological and natural-parameter perturbations.

If a transition is merely an existing module under a new representation, do not count it as a new edge or new portfolio module.

## Decision 23 — Horizon follows persistence, never the reverse

Do not adopt a 60-75 minute hold because outside positive controls used longer holds.

First prove the post-transition state persists; then estimate its natural lifetime; only then design payoff.

## Decision 24 — Macro remains the main parallel information-expanding lane

    scheduled release

    -> actual-consensus surprise when known
    -> first reaction
    -> acceptance/failure

Do not mix macro surprise into the chart-only state model without explicit lane separation.
