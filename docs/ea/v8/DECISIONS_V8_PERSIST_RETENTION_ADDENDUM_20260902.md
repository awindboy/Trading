## D-V8-140 — P15 semantic correction

`P15` is direction-blind first-touch excursion probability. It must not be described as terminal net displacement, sustained movement, or direction persistence.

## D-V8-141 — Persistence must be state-updated

A second probability built at the fresh timestamp from the same pre-fresh 86-feature representation does not provide reliable incremental persistence discrimination inside fresh75. Persistence research therefore updates only after new causal price action is observed.

## D-V8-142 — Equal-distance controls are mandatory

Any claim that a direction reveal predicts continuation must be checked from the current reveal/acceptance price with symmetric favorable/adverse distance. Origin-anchored farther-rung success is not sufficient because it can be dominated by distance asymmetry.

## D-V8-143 — Old PERSIST-A/B momentum claim downgraded

Earlier AUC from `next rung vs pullback failure` is materially confounded by target/failure geometry. It remains useful as a competing-risk execution state but is not authority for generic momentum persistence.

SHORT structural-retention ranking is materially stronger than LONG in current 2025/2026 development evidence. Do not create a SHORT-only or 2026-specific rule. Investigate outcome-blind regime/context explanations first.

## D-V8-147 — RETENTION is not same-direction extension

A high structural-retention score predicts accepted-structure survival, not equal-distance continuation or guaranteed same-direction `0.75 H4 ATR` delivery.
