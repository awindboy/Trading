## Current scientific state

### What is known

- automated Double-B side/body rules are an inadequate representation of the method;
- a 24-event blinded visual pilot failed;
- outcome-informed reverse engineering showed substantial hindsight flexibility;
- the largest conceptual improvement is separating:
  `fresh expansion / range extreme / terminal expansion / insufficient information`;
- KTR works best as a distance coordinate system rather than a fixed multiplier table;
- blind equal-risk-per-leg averaging can create very large campaign losses.

### What is not known

- whether the AI can classify these states causally with useful accuracy;
- whether the user/teacher discretionary process itself has a robust edge;
- whether a formalized V7 can reach WR >=50% and avg positive >=2R;
- whether staged entry improves cost-adjusted expectancy after campaign-risk normalization;
- whether V7 generalizes across independent markets/periods.
