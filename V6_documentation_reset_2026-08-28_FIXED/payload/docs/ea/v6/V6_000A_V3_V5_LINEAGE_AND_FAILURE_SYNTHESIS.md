# V6-000A — V3/V5 Lineage, Failure, and Open-Hypothesis Synthesis

Status: `ACTIVE V6 FOUNDATIONAL AUTHORITY`  
Date: `2026-08-28`  
Production authority: `NONE`

## 1. Purpose

This document prevents V6 from being designed from a shallow reading of the last V3 or V5 result.

V6 must inherit both what survived careful causal research and what failed. The central problem is not `find one more filter` and not `find one external variable that predicts GOLD`.

It is:

```text
why can the same causally meaningful GOLD event
have different directional/path meaning
across periods and environments,
and which causally observable state variables explain that difference?
```

This is lineage authority, not strategy authority.

## 2. V3 — what was actually researched

### 2.1 Research progression

V3 changed the research unit from sparse final EA fills to raw GOLD data and broad causal event populations:

```text
raw GOLD M1
-> causal multi-timeframe structure/liquidity reconstruction
-> broad sweep/reaction populations
-> matched controls and mirror tests
-> reload-state / local-acceptance interaction
-> separate low-R and high-R modules
-> frozen Candidate B
-> untouched GOLD 2022 validation
```

Important negative findings before Candidate B included:

- sweep alone was not directional alpha;
- delayed recovery and looser trigger variants degraded quality;
- mandatory FVG-midpoint interpretations were not supported;
- fixed momentum horizons did not generalize;
- trade-level feature mining did not produce a stable universal winner/loser classifier;
- exact mirrors sometimes matched or beat the intended direction;
- quarter-by-quarter behavior was unstable before 2022 was opened.

Therefore V3 did not simply discover one pattern and immediately call it an edge.

### 2.2 Strongest V3 development object

V3-003C reconstructed a broad causal reload/reaction event:

```text
persistent intermediate liquidity
-> atomic same-M1 sweep/recovery
-> pre-sweep M5 owner opposite reaction
-> sweep extreme remains intact
-> first completed M5 owner transition back with reaction
-> trigger-close Entry
-> sweep-extreme structural SL
```

Broad physical population:

```text
2023 84 events / +1R 51.19%
2024 86 events / +1R 53.49%
2025 67 events / +1R 52.24%
```

Two price-derived states interacted strongly in discovery:

```text
DELIVERY_ACTIVE
= M30 wave expansion > 1
  OR M30 and H1 BOS-owner alignment

STRONG_LOCAL_ACCEPTANCE
= acceptance beyond the broken M5 structure
  > source-liquidity penetration
```

Joint discovery subset:

```text
2023 N40  +1R 60.00%
2024 N29  +1R 65.52%
2025 N27  +1R 62.96%
```

V3 then separated local/high-precision behavior (`Module L`) from large-payoff continuation (`Module H`). Frozen Candidate B on consumed 2023-2025 discovery produced approximately:

```text
N 53
positive 52.83%
avg positive +2.775R
EV +0.994R/trade
```

This was meaningful development evidence, not final generalization evidence.

### 2.3 Independent validation failure was real

Frozen GOLD 2022 validation:

```text
accepted          24
positive           6 = 25.0%
avg positive      +1.458R
EV                -0.385R/trade
total             -9.25R
max negative run   8
```

The failure started upstream of final management:

```text
Candidate-A +1R survival = 17 / 39 = 43.59%
```

Zero-spread replay did not repair survival. Exposure-policy alternatives remained strongly negative.

The relationship itself also weakened or reversed:

- H: original +5R = 1/20, exact mirror +5R = 6/20;
- L: checkpoint = 2/5, mirror checkpoint = 3/5;
- H->L links: discovery 4/5 net-positive vs validation 1/4.

Therefore V3 did not fail only because exits gave back winners or because spread was high.

## 3. What V3 failure proves — and does not prove

It proves:

- Candidate B is not validated strategy authority;
- 2023-2025 development success is insufficient;
- a human-readable state can change predictive meaning across environments;
- mirror/reversal controls are necessary when directional meaning is claimed;
- Entry survival and winner continuation must stay separate;
- a validation reversal must not be repaired with post-hoc thresholds.

It does NOT prove any single explanation such as:

```text
V3 was only noise
V3 failed only because of exit management
V3 failed only because of execution costs
all price structure is useless
one missing indicator will necessarily solve it
```

Competing explanations remain open and may coexist:

1. `SELECTION / MULTIPLICITY OVERFIT`
   - many V3 hypotheses and natural variants were consumed on 2023-2025;
   - Candidate B can contain selection bias even though 2022 was untouched.

2. `COVARIATE SHIFT`
   - P(X) may change by period.

3. `HIDDEN / OMITTED CONTEXT`
   - V3 price/structure X may omit state that changes event meaning.

4. `CONCEPT SHIFT`
   - P(Y|X) may change even for similar observed state.

5. `EVENT-FORMULATION INSUFFICIENCY`
   - the broad event may combine causally different sub-events.

6. `EXECUTION ENVIRONMENT`
   - essential for final economics, but insufficient to explain the main 2022 semantic collapse by itself.

V3 itself had already escalated to the L3 question:

```text
is price-only information sufficient for the objective?
```

That unresolved question is a direct ancestor of V6.

## 4. V5 — what was actually learned

### 4.1 Success-first setup research exposed an economic frontier

Across V5-002..025 the recurring result was:

```text
higher Entry survival -> smaller structural winners
larger multi-R winners -> lower realized hit rate
```

This does NOT mean technical methods are useless. It means setup recognition alone did not jointly solve Entry, invalidation, and payoff geometry under the final target.

### 4.2 First Cross was not simply a no-edge failure

V5-030A development:

```text
N                    406
WR                   53.94%
avg positive NET R    1.197R
EV                   +0.148R/trade
```

It therefore contained development edge under that architecture, but failed the later `avg positive >=2R` requirement.

The payoff audit showed favorable excursion existed:

```text
raw structural MFE >=2R        28.85%
clear +1R trades later >=2R    45.29%
```

Yet the consumed partial-at-1R + BE + same-runner family could not satisfy both final constraints:

```text
max avg positive while WR>=50%  ~1.515R
max WR while avg positive>=2R   ~39.66%
```

The failure was an architecture/economic incompatibility within that lifecycle family, not proof that every First Cross observation was random.

### 4.3 Portable measurement is not portable meaning

V5-036 transferred the exact V3 D-145 M30 protected->external progress measurement into the First Cross +1R population.

Measurement portability passed. Predictive relationship transfer failed and reversed across important environments.

Permanent lesson:

```text
portable observable != portable predictive relationship != portable edge
```

V6 must never assume an indicator has universal meaning merely because it can be computed identically everywhere.

### 4.4 External-state branches require precise interpretation

V5 does NOT establish that external indicators are useless:

- real yield: one specific 2023 next-day directional-delivery hypothesis was falsified; 2024/2025 were not opened;
- source-faithful Williams COT: only 2 qualifying reports versus minimum N=12; price outcome was not opened;
- GLD holdings flow: prepared but not completed; no empirical classification.

What failed strategically was drifting toward replacing the central V3 problem with unrelated standalone variable hunting.

External, macro, positioning, flow, or cross-market information remains eligible in V6 only when it measures a preregistered missing-context hypothesis around the event/problem under study.

## 5. V4 boundary inherited by V6

V4 moved from hand-authored state to generic causal raw-sequence learning. Practical project work did not produce useful learned signal.

Therefore V6 must not assume:

```text
raw data + larger/newer model = solved representation
```

Use event conditioning and establish causal information/measurement value before escalating representation complexity.

## 6. V6 design consequences

### 6.1 Indicators are measurements of context

`Indicator` in V6 means any causally available measurement intended to represent a specific market-state dimension. It may derive from:

- GOLD price/structure across timescales;
- volatility, range, compression/expansion, activity and liquidity proxies;
- spread/execution state;
- related-market prices and relative relationships;
- rates/macro state;
- positioning or fund/flow state;
- scheduled-event or market-environment state when causally available.

RSI, MACD, ATR, a yield series, COT, XAUEUR, or a learned embedding has no privileged status. The research question must state what latent state it is supposed to measure.

### 6.2 Do not build an indicator tournament

V6 may not:

```text
test dozens of indicators
-> choose the best year/threshold/transformation
-> call the survivor a regime model
```

Each family requires before outcome inspection:

- mechanism/state hypothesis;
- exact causal availability;
- fixed transformation set;
- target research stage;
- matched/null control;
- chronological environments;
- kill condition;
- family-level experiment budget.

A failed family may be closed and a genuinely different preregistered family opened later. That is different from rescuing a consumed experiment.

### 6.3 Initial anchor policy

The exact V3-003C broad population is the initial V6 event anchor because it is causal and does not bake later Candidate-A/B gates into selection.

Expected parity:

```text
2023 84
2024 86
2025 67
```

V6 may later redefine the event only in a separate preregistered phase if `EVENT-FORMULATION INSUFFICIENCY` becomes the active hypothesis.

### 6.4 Stage-specific meaning

A variable discovered for Entry -> +1R survival does not automatically become a continuation/exit variable.

A variable discovered for +1R -> +2R continuation does not automatically become an Entry filter.
